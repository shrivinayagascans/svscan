MAMMOGRAPHY
Karur's First Digital Mammography , and it's a special form of imaging system that users an x-ray to examine breasts. It helps in the early diagnosis hence guides treatment of breast diseases. Mammography is an equipment which uses x-rays and obtains images of the breast that can be produced on a computer screen or on special film similar to conventional mammograms. This helps in future reference as the images are stored electronically. Additional software’s are available.
ADVANCED CT SCAN - 80 SLICE
Karur's First 80 slice Advanced CT scan machine and Our CT scanning sometimes called CAT scanning is a noninvasive medical test that helps physicians diagnose and treat medical conditions. Our CT scanning combines special X-ray equipment with sophisticated computers to produce multiple images or pictures of the inside of the body. These cross-sectional images of the area being studied can then be examined on a computer monitor.
