Our Services

An MRI Scanner consists of a large and very strong magnet in which the patient lies. A radio wave antenna is used to send signals to the body and then receive signals back. These returning signals are converted into pictures by a computer attached to the scanner. Pictures of almost any part of your body can be obtained at almost any particular angle and plane. The triple-layered icing on the cake is, MRI has absolutely no side effects, it's completely painless and is non-invasive as well.

Our CT scanning sometimes called CAT scanning is a noninvasive medical test that helps physicians diagnose and treat medical conditions. Our CT scanning combines special X-ray equipment with sophisticated computers to produce multiple images or pictures of the inside of the body. These cross-sectional images of the area being studied can then be examined on a computer monitor, printed or transferred to a CD. Our CT scans of internal organs, bones, soft tissue, and blood vessels provide greater clarity and reveal more details than regular X-ray exams.

The Ultrasound imaging in SVS DIAGNOSTICS is a noninvasive medical test that helps physicians diagnose and treat medical conditions. Conventional ultrasound displays the images in thin, flat sections of the body. Advancements in ultrasound technology include three-dimensional (3D) images. Four-dimensional (4D) ultrasound is 3D ultrasound in motion. A Doppler ultrasound study may be part of an ultrasound examination. The Doppler ultrasound in SVS DIAGNOSTICS is a special ultrasound technique that evaluates blood flow through a blood vessel including the body's major arteries and veins in the abdomen, arms, legs, and neck.

  • An echocardiogram, or 'echo', is a scan used to look at the heart and nearby blood vessels. It's a type of ultrasound scan, which means a small probe is used to send out high-frequency sound waves that create echoes when they bounce off different parts of the body.
  • An echocardiogram uses sound waves to produce images of your heart. This common test allows your doctor to see your heart beating and pumping blood. Your doctor can use the images from an echocardiogram to identify heart disease.

SVS DIAGNOSTICS Digital Mammography is a special form of imaging system that uses an x-ray to examine breasts. It helps in the early diagnosis, hence guides treatment of breast diseases. Mammography is an equipment which uses x-rays and obtains images of the breast that can be produced on a computer screen or on special film similar to conventional mammograms. This helps in future reference as the images are stored electronically. Additional software's are available through which the doctor can zoom the image, have a closer look and hence arrive at a precise diagnosis.
